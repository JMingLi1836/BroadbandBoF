# Name: Broadband

# Description

The broadband networking landscape is evolving, driven by increasing service diversity, growing operational requirements, and the emergence of new services such as cloud and AI-based applications. However, broadband-related protocols and technologies are currently developed and maintained across multiple IETF working groups, which may lead to conflicts or overlaps among the resulting standards.

Many existing broadband protocols and mechanisms, including PPPoE, IPoE-based access, and ANCP, remain widely deployed in operational networks but face challenges in meeting evolving requirements. Operators and vendors have introduced various extensions and implementation-specific solutions to address these challenges. However, the lack of a dedicated standardization place makes it difficult to bring these solutions into the IETF standardization process, potentially leading to fragmented implementations and interoperability issues.

This BOF aims to discuss the need for a dedicated Broadband Working Group within the IETF. The proposed WG would provide a place to collect operational use cases and requirements, analyze gaps in existing broadband protocols and architectures, coordinate protocol evolution across relevant IETF working groups, and develop broadband-specific extensions or new mechanisms where needed.

The BOF will also discuss the proposed scope and initial work items, the relationship with existing IETF working groups and external standardization bodies such as the Broadband Forum (BBF), and whether there is sufficient interest, expertise, and commitment from operators, vendors, and other stakeholders to establish the proposed working group.
